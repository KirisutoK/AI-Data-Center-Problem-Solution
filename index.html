The Energy Problem behind Artificial Intelligence

What AI data centers mean for our power, water, and neighborhoods, and how our community can respond.

Who this site is for

Welcome! This site is for local residents and government officials who want to understand AI data centers: large facilities full of specialized computers that store and process the data AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini depend on. Here you will learn what AI data centers are, how they affect electricity, heat, and water in a community, and what we can do about it.

Key facts at a glance

Fact 1

Electricity demand is climbing

Data centers are among the fastest-growing electricity users in the U.S., and their demand is projected to rise 7–12% by 2030 (Knittel et al., 2025, p. 1).

Fact 2

Trillions in new investment

The world is projected to need $6.7 trillion in data center spending by 2030, with $5.2 trillion of it for AI-ready facilities (Ginzburg-Ganz et al., 2026, p. 18).

Fact 3

Communities are pushing back

More than 140 local groups have blocked or delayed over $60 billion in data center projects in roughly a year (Yale School of the Environment, 2026).
