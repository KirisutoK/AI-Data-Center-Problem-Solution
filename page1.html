What Is an AI Data Center?

What it is

An AI data center is a building filled with powerful computers that store, process, and move huge amounts of data. It uses specialized hardware that lets AI analyze information in real time, which is how chatbots and AI tutors can answer in seconds (Cisco, n.d.). Think of it as AI's engine room: every time someone uses an AI tool, a data center somewhere does the work.

Why more are being built

As more people use AI for school, work, and everyday tasks, companies need more data centers to keep up. U.S. data center electricity demand is projected to grow 7–12% by 2030 (Knittel et al., 2025, p. 1), and the world may need $6.7 trillion in data center spending by then (Ginzburg-Ganz et al., 2026, p. 18). That means new facilities could be proposed in towns like ours.

Key terms

Power grid

The network of power plants and power lines that delivers electricity to the homes and businesses in an area.

Cooling system

Servers produce intense heat, so data centers use cooling systems, often water-based, to keep their high-density server racks from overheating (Ginzburg-Ganz et al., 2026, p. 20).
